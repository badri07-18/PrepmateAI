# 🚀 PrepMate AI

View your app in : https://prepmate-ai.ai.studio

**AI-Powered Placement Preparation Platform**

PrepMate AI is a web-based platform designed to help students prepare for placements through personalized assessments, coding practice, aptitude preparation, resume analysis, mock interviews, communication practice, and AI-powered learning roadmaps.

## 🎯 Problem

Students often use multiple platforms for placement preparation, making it difficult to track their skills, identify weak areas, and follow a personalized preparation plan.

## 💡 Solution

PrepMate AI brings essential placement-preparation activities into one platform and provides personalized guidance based on the student's performance, skills, and career goals.

## ✨ Features

* 🔐 Student Login & Profile
* 📊 AI-Based Skill Assessment
* 🧠 Technical, Aptitude & Communication Assessment
* 💻 Coding Practice
* 📄 Resume Analysis

## 📊 Personalized Learning

PrepMate AI analyzes student performance and identifies areas that need improvement. The roadmap can adapt based on:

* Assessment performance
* Technical skills
* Aptitude performance
* Communication skills
* Coding performance
* Resume quality
* Target career role

## 💻 Coding Practice

Students can practice coding problems and submit their solutions against test cases. Successful submissions are tracked as part of their preparation progress.

## 📄 Resume Analyzer

The Resume Analyzer evaluates important resume sections such as:

* Education
* Skills
* Projects
* Internships
* Certifications
* Contact information
* Relevant keywords

It provides a score and improvement suggestions based on the resume content.

## 🎤 Mock Interview

The mock interview module helps students practice interview questions and receive feedback on their responses.
